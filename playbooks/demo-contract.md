# Synthetic Demo Contract

Parties: Acme Widgets, Inc. and Northwind Services LLC (both fictional).
This contract is synthetic and written only for demos and tests. No real
client, person, or transaction is described here.

## 1. Confidentiality

Confidential Information means non-public information disclosed in written
or electronic form. The receiving party will protect Confidential
Information in perpetuity and its confidentiality duty is perpetual
confidentiality without any time limit.

## 2. Services and Payment

Northwind will provide implementation services. The client will pay fees
stated in each order. Each invoice is payable Net 60 after receipt. Payment
is due only after an invoice is issued.

## 3. Term and Termination

The term of this agreement is one year. Either party may terminate for
convenience on ten days written notice.

## 4. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas. Any dispute
will be resolved in the state courts located in Texas.

## 5. Restrictive Covenants

The employee accepts a non-compete duty: for twenty-four months after
employment ends, the employee shall not work for any competitor anywhere
in the world.

## 6. Liability

Each party accepts unlimited liability for all claims arising under this
Agreement, and liability shall be unlimited without any cap. Compensation
for services is the salary and fees stated in Section 2.
